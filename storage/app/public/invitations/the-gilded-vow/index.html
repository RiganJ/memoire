Our Story

Every love story is beautiful. This one is ours.

Chapter 012022

First Meet

A simple hello became a conversation we never wanted to end.

Our story began on an ordinary afternoon, in a moment neither of us knew would become extraordinary. A brief introduction grew into hours of effortless conversation, shared laughter, and the quiet feeling that we had known each other far longer than we actually had.

Looking back, it was not a grand beginning. It was something gentler—and perhaps more beautiful: two lives quietly finding their way toward one another.

Chapter 022023

First Date

Coffee, laughter, and the quiet certainty that something special had begun.

There was no elaborate plan—just a warm café, two cups of coffee, and stories exchanged until the evening lights came on. Time seemed to move differently that day. Every small detail felt vivid, from the music in the room to the way we both kept finding reasons to stay a little longer.

Chapter 0318 · 01 · 2026

The Proposal

One question, one joyful yes, and a promise to choose each other always.

Surrounded by the warmth of a day we will always remember, a question was asked and answered with the happiest yes. It was intimate, sincere, and perfectly ours—the moment every road behind us became one shared path ahead.

That evening, we celebrated not only an engagement, but the life we were ready to build together.

Chapter 0418 · 01 · 2027

The Wedding

The first page of our forever, celebrated with the people we love.

On this day, before our families and dearest friends, we will make the promises that carry us into forever. Every smile, every embrace, and every joyful tear will become part of the story we tell for years to come.

More than a celebration, this is our way of saying thank you—to love, to time, and to everyone who helped guide us home to each other.
